Portfolios of these partnerships are now about 80%&nbsp;comparable, but there is some difference due to securities and cash becoming available at varying times, payments made to partners, etc. Over the past few years, there hasn't been any partnership which has consistently been at the top or bottom of performance from year to year, and the variance is narrowing as the portfolios tend to become comparable.</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">幸运的是，我们在1959年取得了相对良好的业绩。全年运营的六个合伙企业实现了22.3%到30.0%不等的整体净收益，平均约为25.9%。现在这些合伙企业的投资组合中大约有80%是可比的，但由于每家企业的证券和现金的可动用时点不同，以及支付给合伙人款项等原因，合伙企业间的回报率仍存在一些差异。在过去几年中，没有任何一个合伙企业的表现始终最好或最差。随着投资组合趋于可比，合伙企业间的差异也正在缩小。</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">The overall net gain is determined on the basis of market values at the beginning and end of the year adjusted for payments made to partners or contributions received from them. It is not based on actual realized profits during the year, but is intended to measure the change in liquidating value for the year. It is before interest allowed to partners (where that is specified in the partnership agreement) and before any division of profit to the general partner, but after operating expenses.</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">整体净收益是基于年初和年末的市场价值决定，并依据支付给合伙人的款项或收到合伙人付款作出调整。这个收益不是基于年度内实际实现利润，而是旨在衡量年度内清算价值的变化。它尚未扣除应付给合伙人的利息（如果合伙协议中有规定的话），也尚未将任何利润分配给普通合伙人，但是已经扣除了运营费用。</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;color: rgb(64, 118, 0);">【译者注：本段简记，整体净收益：+净收益+收到合伙人款项-支付合伙人款项-（待减未减的应付利息）-运营费用】</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">The principal operating expense is the Nebraska Intangibles Tax which amounts to .4% of market value on practically all securities. Last year represented the first time that this tax had been effectively enforced and, of course penalized our results to the extent of .4%.</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">主要运营费用是内布拉斯加州征收的无形资产税，这项税几乎对所有证券按其市场价值的0.4%征收。该税种在去年首次执行，当然也使我们的业绩表现降低了0.4%。</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><strong><span style="font-size: 16px;">The present portfolio:</span></strong><o:p></o:p></section><h4 style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><strong><span style="font-size: 16px;">当前投资组合：</span></strong></h4><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Last year, I mentioned a new commitment which involved about 25% of assets of the various partnerships. Presently this investment is about 35% of assets. This is an unusually large percentage, but has been made for strong reasons. In effect, this company is partially an investment trust owing some thirty or forty other securities of high quality. Our investment was made and is carried at a substantial discount from asset value based on market value of their securities and a conservative appraisal of the operating business.</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">去年，我提到了一项新的投资承诺，约涉及各合伙企业资产的25%。目前这项投资约占全部合伙企业资产的35%。这个比例异常之大，但我们有充分的理由进行这项投资。实际上，这家公司有一部分是一个投资信托，拥有三四十种高质量证券。</span><span style="font-size: 16px;letter-spacing: 0.578px;Helvetica Neue&quot;, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei UI&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, sans-serif;">我们的投资决策和账面价值计算，既基于按市场价值对这部分证券资产进行较大折扣后的评估，也基于对该公司主营业务的保守评估。</span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;color: rgb(64, 118, 0);">【译者注：上面提到的</span><span style="color: rgb(64, 118, 0);font-size: 16px;Helvetica Neue&quot;, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei UI&quot;, &quot;Microsoft with our ideas. The probability is extremely high that the performance of this investment will be superior to that of the general market until its disposition, and I am hopeful that this will take place this year.</span><o:p></o:p></section><section style="margin-top: 0px;margin-bottom: 0px;line-height: 1.6em;text-align: left;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br  /></span></section><section
